## 👋 About This Repository

Welcome to my **Certificates & Achievements** repository!

This repository is my digital archive of certificates, credentials,
participation records, internships, badges, and academic milestones. It
reflects my continuous journey of learning, exploring new technologies,
developing practical skills, and participating in opportunities beyond
the classroom.

I maintain this collection to keep my achievements organized and make it
easy to revisit my learning journey.
